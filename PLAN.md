# Decidr — The Debate Settling App

## What We're Building

Decidr is a sleek, dark-themed social app where people settle arguments by presenting both sides and letting the community (and an AI Judge) vote on who's right.

---

### **Features**

- **Create a Decision**: Write a debate title, then present Side A and Side B with text descriptions
- **Browse Decisions**: Scroll through a feed of active debates sorted by trending/newest
- **Vote on Decisions**: Tap to vote for Side A or Side B — your vote is final
- **AI Judge (Flagship Feature)**: Every decision gets an AI-powered verdict with detailed reasoning — but it stays locked until you vote first
- **AI Reveal Animation**: After voting, the AI Judge section unlocks with a dramatic gavel animation and reveals the AI's vote + reasoning
- **Live Vote Tallies**: See real-time percentage split between both sides with animated progress bars
- **Justifications**: After voting, leave an optional short comment explaining your choice
- **User Profile**: See your stats — decisions created, votes cast, and AI alignment rate
- **Categories & Search**: Browse by category (Food, Lifestyle, Politics, Tech, etc.) and search decisions
- **Share Decisions**: Share debates externally with a tap

---

### **Design**

- **Dark theme** inspired by X/Twitter — deep charcoal backgrounds with layered card surfaces
- **Coral (#FF6B6B) for Side A** and **Cyan (#4ECDC4) for Side B** — the two sides always have distinct, vibrant colors
- **Split-view cards** showing both sides at a glance with clean typography
- **Animated vote bars** that fill with smooth spring animations when results are revealed
- **Gavel icon animation** for the AI Judge reveal — scales up with a bounce effect
- **Micro-interactions**: Button press haptics, card press animations, smooth page transitions
- **Premium feel**: Subtle gradients on cards, soft shadows, generous spacing
- **San Francisco-style typography**: Clean hierarchy with bold titles and lighter body text

---

### **Screens**

1. **Home Feed** — Scrollable feed of decision cards with category filters at the top, search bar, and a floating "+" button to create new decisions
2. **Decision Detail** — Full debate view with Side A vs Side B in a balanced split layout, vote buttons at the bottom, locked AI Judge section that reveals after voting, and a comments/justifications section
3. **Create Decision** — Step-by-step form: title, category, Side A argument, Side B argument — clean and focused
4. **Profile** — User stats dashboard with decisions created, votes cast, AI alignment badge, and a list of your decisions
5. **Discover** — Browse by category, trending topics, and search
